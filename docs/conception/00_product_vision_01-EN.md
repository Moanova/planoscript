# Planoscript :: Vision produit


## Planoscript : A visual narrative map editor for creators of complex stories.
- **Description**:
    Planoscript is the tool that enables screenwriters of video games, micro-series and other forms of modern fiction to
      - visually build their narrative maps using drag-and-drop without any technical skills,
      - manage branching paths and narrative arcs with a clear view of the links between elements,
      - export their work into universal formats to collaborate with their teams (developers, directors) without manual operations.
    Why?
    Traditional word processors are unsuitable, and specialist professional applications are either too complex or too expensive.
    No solution exactly meets the needs of modern fiction creators, particularly independent ones.
    1. Flexibility: suited to complex, non-linear narratives.
    2. Simplicity: an intuitive, code-free interface so you can focus on structuring your stories.
    3. Collaboration: simplified sharing with teams via directly usable exports.
    In a nutshell, Planoscript is the visual Swiss Army knife for independent screenwriters, enabling them to structure complex narratives without getting bogged down in technicalities.
