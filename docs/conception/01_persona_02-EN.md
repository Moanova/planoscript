# Planoscript :: Persona 01 - TomSon


##Tom Son, micro-series writer
- **Description**:
    Aged 32, lead screenwriter for a production company specialising in micro-series, with 7 years’ experience, including 4 in short series for mobile platforms, accustomed to working on projects with tight budgets and tight deadlines.
    His current project involves writing a new romantic fiction piece for TikTok.
    He is collaborating with two screenwriters and a director.
- **Challenges**:
    Microseries can feature numerous characters with complex narrative arcs in tightly structured stories full of twists and turns, which maintain constant tension to hold the audience’s attention.
    Maintaining plot consistency over the long term is therefore a fundamental challenge, particularly when it comes to relationships between characters.
    It is also necessary to ensure a steady stream of plot twists to captivate the audience.
    Finally, given budget and deadline constraints, it is also essential to optimise workflow efficiency using the most appropriate tools.
- **Objectives**:
    1. To visually structure modular narrative frameworks in a flexible manner.
    2. To organise multiple versions in a straightforward way so as to retain all options until a final decision is made.
    3. To clearly establish the nature of the relationships between the characters.
    4. To clearly establish the current situation at the start of each episode and each season.
- **Frustrations**:
    1. The tools used so far are unsuitable and too basic (Google Docs, Google Sheets).
    2. They do not allow us to easily track how situations develop across episodes (when was a particular piece of information shared with a particular character?)
    3. Professional tools are beyond his budget and too complex (Final Draft, Arc Studio Pro). They require too long a learning curve and are over-specced for his needs.
- **Constraints**:
    1. Time constraints: the time available must be used for the actual writing, not for organising the work using unsuitable tools.
    2. Budget constraints: purchasing an expensive software licence is not currently feasible.
- **Typical quote**:
    "I really thought we were going to have to cancel the next season because we couldn’t tie up all the loose ends from the previous ones.
    I need a tool to organise all the story arcs efficiently without spending hours searching through my Google Docs folders."
- **Technical skills**:
    Standard office software, but no coding skills. The ideal tool would be an intuitive drag-and-drop utility that works on Windows and macOS.
